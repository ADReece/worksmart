It lifts your screen to eye level, clears desk space, and lets you position your display exactly where it reduces eye and neck strain. ## How to pick the right monitor arm

- Check your monitor’s VESA size (the mounting pattern on the back—commonly 75x75 or 100x100 mm).
- Confirm your desk can support clamping or grommet mounting; hollow-core desks may need extra reinforcement.
- Make sure the arm’s weight capacity covers your monitor plus any attached peripherals.

If you’re still hunched over a low screen or fighting cable clutter, a monitor arm is one of those small changes that makes your desk feel like it was built for you instead of against you.
